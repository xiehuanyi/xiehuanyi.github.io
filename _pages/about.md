I am an MS/PhD student in Computer Science at **King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST)**, advised by **Prof. Di Wang**.

My current work focuses on efficient training and inference of large language models, especially **LLM serving**, **memory-efficient fine-tuning**, and **data attribution**.

Previously, I received my M.Sc. in Data Science from **KTH Royal Institute of Technology** and my B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from **Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai**.

I also build small open-source systems for understanding modern LLM infrastructure:

- [nanoSGLang](https://github.com/xiehuanyi/nanoSGLang): a minimal LLM serving engine with continuous batching, paged KV cache, chunked prefill, and streaming inference.
- [nanoMegatron](https://github.com/xiehuanyi/nanoMegatron): a minimal distributed training framework covering DDP, ZeRO, tensor parallelism, pipeline parallelism, and expert parallelism.

You can find more details in my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) or on [GitHub](https://github.com/xiehuanyi).
